## Context

**What it is.**

Future demand realisations known. The ceiling on everything.

**Why it is worth having as well as B-state.**

Used as the **denominator**, so improvements report on a 0–100% scale rather than as raw dollars that
depend entirely on an assumed cost ratio.

Reporting the gain as a fraction of (B-clairvoyant − P0) does one specific job:

> It separates **information about age** from **information about demand**, and it keeps the headline
> from being an artefact of the assumed stockout penalty.

Given that demand is handed to every policy as a known distribution, the gap between B-state and
B-clairvoyant is precisely the value of *forecasting*, which makes it the natural yardstick for the
project's own thesis that forecasting is not ordering.

**Why in or out.**

**Out:** if the headline is denominated in profit and the cost assumptions are stated, the fractional
scale is a nicety.

> **Recommended: In.** Also near-free, and it pre-empts "your number is just your cost assumptions."
