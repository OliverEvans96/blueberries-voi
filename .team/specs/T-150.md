# T-150 — f-native hierarchical arrival freshness model

Decision of record: **[ADR 0144](../adr/0144-f-native-hierarchical-arrival-model.md)**
(supersedes [0138](../adr/0138-arrival-f-birth-wiring.md) and
[0141](../adr/0141-unified-gamma-arrival-model.md)). Read it before writing tests or code — the
`k·θ·η_ref = 1` recalibration and the "no channel observes `f`" invariant are the two things
most likely to be violated by accident.

## Process

TDD is binding. **qa writes RED tests before any production code**, per phase, and records the
criterion → test map in **`.team/qa/T-150-tests.md`**. The verify verdict goes to
`.team/qa/T-150.md`. Implementation lands as **three commits, one per phase, in order**; never
mix a rename with a semantic change in the same commit.

Phase 1 is mechanical and must leave the suite green with **no edited numeric expectation**.
Phase 2 changes physics and is expected to move goldens. Phase 3 changes the wire, the frontend,
and the calibrated constants.

### Correction round 2 (2026-08-22, r3) — AC2.11a is restated, not relaxed. Binding on qa.

The physics correction landed. On `team/T-150/fix-physics-implement` at `6badc66` the artifact is
physically anchored (`sigma_T = 0.4`, `delay_scale = 1.0`, `mix_weight` deleted), the prior mean
`f` is 0.531 against a target of ≈0.53, and eight of the nine correction-round tests pass. The
ninth, `ac2_11a_empirical_ladder_tracking_mae`, passes its ordering assertion
(`MAE(F3) = 0.0423 < MAE(F2) = 0.0468 < MAE(P0) = 0.1229`) and fails its magnitude assertion:
`MAE(P0) / MAE(F2) = 2.63` against a required `3.0`.

The implementer recommended relaxing the 3× threshold. **That recommendation is rejected.** The
threshold is not what is broken. The *estimator* is. Ruling below; the reasoning is recorded at
length because Risk 4 of this spec cuts both ways, and a reviewer must be able to see that this
criterion was changed because the measurement was wrong, not because the number was inconvenient.

**1. Was the 3× ever derived? No — it was an eyeballed margin, and it is now derived.**
The ratio entered in architect commit `0183fc3`, the correction-round commit that created
AC2.11a, in the same sentence as the indicative magnitudes `MAE(P0) ≈ 0.13`, `MAE(F2) ≈ 0.03`,
`MAE(F3) ≈ 0.01`. Their ratio is 4.33, and "at least 3×" was a ~30% margin under it. No
derivation is recorded anywhere in the spec or in ADR 0144, because there was none: it was an
indicative magnitude presented as a gate. That is the same class of error as AC2.10 — asserting a
number about this project's data as though it were a property of probability — and it is
acknowledged here as such.

The threshold does, however, have a derivation available, and it lands on the same number. To
first order in a linearization of `E[f | Λ]` in `log Λ`, a rung that learns `d` removes the
duration share of `Var(log Λ)` from the between-lot prediction error, so
`sd(signal_P0) / sd(signal_F2) ≈ 1 / √(1 − duration_share)`. AC2.18 item 4 already gates
`duration_share ≥ 0.90` under the artifact's own laws, against an observed 0.984. A ratio gate of
**3.0 is exactly the ladder-side image of a duration share of `1 − 1/9 = 0.889`** — marginally
*weaker* than AC2.18's own floor, therefore consistent with it rather than a second free
parameter. This is why the threshold stays at 3.0 and is neither relaxed to 2.5 to fit the
measurement nor raised to 5 to fit the theory.

**2. Is a ratio the right shape of criterion? Yes, and the ordering alone is not enough.**
Ordering is satisfiable by an arbitrarily small margin, so it does not catch the *near-flat*
ladder — the regression this ticket exists to fix, seen at reduced amplitude. Concretely: the
Phase 2 artifact with `delay_scale = 0.30` squeezed the duration share to 23%. Under an artifact
like that, P0's own prior over `d` is nearly as tight as F2's conditional, so F2's advantage
collapses toward nothing while the *ordering* can still hold by luck on one seed. A magnitude gate
is the only assertion in this spec that fails on that artifact from the *realized ladder* side.
AC2.18 catches the same defect from the *parameter* side. Two independent gates on the defect that
already shipped once is proportionate, not redundant.

**3. Why the measurement cannot resolve it — three contaminations, all of them additive across
rungs and all of them compressing the ratio.**

- **(A) Within-lot sampling noise at 8 units per delivery — dominant.** The test's truth target is
  the *realized* mean `f` of an 8-unit lot; the belief is `E[f | observation]`. No rung observes
  `ψ_pos` or the per-unit gamma (ADR 0144 §5), so `E[f | Λ]` is the Bayes-optimal predictor of that
  realized mean and its error cannot fall below the sampling noise of an 8-unit average. This
  spec's own §Interfaces table gives within-lot `sd(f) ≈ 0.13` at `Λ = 6.5`; `0.13/√8 = 0.046`,
  and the expected absolute deviation of a near-Gaussian mean is `√(2/π) · 0.046 ≈ 0.037`.
  **Measured `MAE(F3) = 0.0423.`** F3's entire error budget is accounted for by the noise floor.
- **(B) The two sides of the comparison are not the same quantity.** `live_lots[*].mean_f`
  (`session.rs:525-533`) filters `f > 0.0` and divides by the *alive* count from `alive_by_lot`
  (`day_step.rs:61-71`), so the truth side is `E[f | f > 0]` — the `f = 0` atom is dropped from
  numerator and denominator alike. `filter_law_mean_f` (`arrival.rs:590-593`) is documented as
  including the atom, as AC2.19 item 4 requires. Whenever one unit of eight arrives dead the truth
  side jumps by a factor of `8/7`, roughly `+0.07`; at a fleet atom near 2% that is `≈0.010` of
  same-signed error added to **every** rung.
- **(C) F3's `Λ` is a reconstruction, not the session's observation.**
  `collect_truth_deliveries` re-draws the delivery through `draw_truth_delivery` on
  independently spawned streams and hardcodes `"abdella_all"`, rather than reading the trace the
  session recorded. It agrees today only because the default corridor and the stream names happen
  to match. Any drift in draw order degrades F3 alone, silently, and the test would read as an F3
  model defect.

Decompose the measurement under `sd(residual) ≈ MAE / √(2/π)`, with the noise term common to all
three rungs and independent of the signal. Taking F3's own signal as zero (it is the exact
conditional mean) gives a noise floor of `sd ≈ 0.053`, and then
`sd(signal_F2) = √(0.0587² − 0.0530²) ≈ 0.025` and
`sd(signal_P0) = √(0.1540² − 0.0530²) ≈ 0.145`.
**The signal ratio is ≈ 5.8.** The measured 2.63 is that same ratio seen through a 0.053 noise
floor. As a consistency check, `1/√(1 − share) = 5.8` implies an effective duration share of
0.970, which is what one expects once `pack_date_days = duration_d.round()` puts roughly `1/12 d²`
of variance back into F2's duration — the numbers hang together to the accuracy of a first-order
argument, and they sit far above 3.0 on both routes.

The implementer's alternative basis — comparing against the unconditional draw mean instead of
session `mean_f` — is **wrong on principle regardless of the number it produces**, and it is
fortunate that it produced a worse one. That basis compares a belief against a model-derived
quantity, so an artifact that is internally coherent but wholly disconnected from the truth path
would pass. AC2.11/AC2.11a exist precisely to catch belief decoupling from realized truth, so the
realized truth state is the only admissible target. **The basis stays session-realized truth.**
What changes is that the realization must be large enough for the estimator to resolve the effect,
and the two sides must be the same quantity.

**4. Ruling: restate AC2.11a.** Keep the ordering, keep the ratio at 3.0, fix the estimator, and
add a data-anchored guard on F3 that is sharper than anything the ordering can express. Precise
new text is below; precise qa instructions are in §qa changes required. The three versions
considered:

| Version | Catches | Misses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep as written | nothing more than the restatement | fails on a correct model; forces exactly the tune-the-artifact-to-the-test failure Risk 4 names, in mirror image |
| Withdraw the ratio, keep ordering | the fully flat ladder | the near-flat ladder — a `delay_scale`-style regression that keeps the ordering by luck |
| **Restate (adopted)** | flat and near-flat ladder; F3 regressing to a biased predictor (e.g. reverting to the `φ̄`-divided cache); like-for-like drift between truth and belief | a regression that degrades all three rungs by the same additive amount — which AC2.18 and AC2.19 own |

**Finding on the F3-to-F2 gap: no defect in F3. The gap is the expected consequence of measuring
a 0.025 effect with a 0.053 ruler.** The user's instinct is right that knowing `Λ` exactly ought
to beat knowing only the pack date by more than 0.0045, and it does — by ≈0.025 in prediction-error
terms, which is 5.5× the observed MAE difference. Two things floor both rungs at a similar level,
and they are contaminations (A) and (B) above, not the `ψ_pos` spread and the gamma decrement
"dominating the residual error" in any sense that implicates F3: those two *are* the noise floor,
they are supposed to be irreducible (no rung observes them, by ADR 0144 §5), and F3 already
attains the floor. This finding is **falsifiable and qa must falsify or confirm it**, by the
n-scaling diagnostic below. If `MAE(F3)` does not fall as `1/√n`, F3 carries a real bias, that
outranks everything in this section, and Phase 2 reopens again.

- [ ] **AC2.11 — restored `bc26218` assertions on a heterogeneous fleet, extended to F3.**
      F2 `f_marginals` differ from P0; a caught-up F2 posterior does not collapse to P0; P0 and
      P1 posteriors differ. All three are restored as strict assertions (they were weakened or
      deleted in commit `bc26218` in `crates/voi_core/src/session.rs` tests). Extended, because
      this is now the only ladder guard in the spec, with a **tracking** assertion that is
      robust rather than a variance assertion that is not (see AC2.11a). All of it is an
      empirical statement about the committed artifact on a realized trajectory, not an

- [ ] **AC2.11a — the ladder tracks truth in the right order, measured with a ruler fine enough
      to see it.** *Restated in correction round 2; the earlier wording is superseded, and the
      indicative magnitudes it quoted were noise-free figures that an 8-unit lot mean cannot
      reach. Read the r3 section above before writing this test.*

      **Fixture.** One truth trajectory, at least 20 deliveries, **at least 64 units per
      delivery**. The unit count is load-bearing, not incidental: the estimator's noise floor is
      `√(2/π) · sd_within / √n`, so at `n = 8` it is ≈0.038 and swamps a ≈0.025 effect, while at
      `n = 64` it is ≈0.013 and the effect is resolved with margin. qa may raise `n` further if
      runtime allows; it may not lower it.

      **One trajectory, three masks — replayed, not re-drawn.** The truth arrival draw is
      mask-invariant by construction (`session.rs:290-309` spawns `STREAM_ARRIVAL_*` from
      `(seed, "session", day)` with no mask input, and delivery is appended *after* aging and
      allocation in `unit_day_step_with_birth`, `day_step.rs:250-272`, so the arrival lot is
      pristine at the snapshot that follows its own step). Step the session once, then read the
      per-rung observations off `events_value(0)` under each `set_obs_scenario`, which applies
      `mask_active` to the recorded `RichDay`. Do **not** reconstruct the delivery by re-drawing
      from `draw_truth_delivery`: that reintroduces contamination (C) and turns any drift in draw
      order into a false F3 defect. Assert the replay is genuine — the per-delivery truth targets
      collected under P0, F2 and F3 must be **bit-identical**.

      **Truth target — atom-inclusive.** Per delivery, `truth_mean_f = Σ(f_values) /
      deliver_units`, where `f_values` comes from `live_lots[last]`. Do **not** use
      `live_lots[*].mean_f`: it filters `f > 0.0` and divides by the alive count, so it is
      `E[f | f > 0]`, whereas `filter_law_mean_f` includes the atom. Assert
      `f_values.len() ≤ deliver_units`, the shortfall being exactly the `f = 0` atom.

      `deliver_units` is **`DayDelta.arrivals` from the `step` return value**, not the order the
      fixture issued. `advance_one` (`session.rs:280-285`) applies `case_round_ceil` and zeroes the
      order when `schedule.can_order` is false, and `pending` accumulates across days, so the order
      quantity is not the delivered count. Getting this denominator wrong biases every rung by the
      same factor and would look exactly like the ratio problem this round is fixing. Note also
      that `advance_one` panics at day 90, which caps how far `n` and the delivery count can be
      pushed together.

      **Belief per rung.** `filter_law_mean_f` on `ArrivalCondition::Prior` (P0),
      `::Duration(pack_date_days)` from the F2-masked log, and `::Exposure(Λ)` with `Λ` from
      `resolve_arrival_exposure` over the F3-masked `temp_times_d` / `temp_temps_c` — the
      production resolver, on the observed trace.

      **Assertions.**
      1. `MAE(F3) < MAE(F2) < MAE(P0)`, **strict**.
      2. `MAE(P0) ≥ 3.0 · MAE(F2)`. Threshold **unchanged**; it is the ladder-side image of
         AC2.18 item 4's `duration_share ≥ 0.90` floor (see r3 §1). Expected ≈5 at `n = 64`.
      3. **F3 attains its Bayes floor.** Compute the floor from the realized lots themselves, not
         from a constant: per delivery, `sd_within` = sample sd of that lot's atom-inclusive unit
         `f` values (the `f_values` plus `deliver_units − f_values.len()` zeros); then
         `floor = mean over deliveries of √(2/π) · sd_within / √deliver_units`. Assert
         `MAE(F3) ≤ 1.5 · floor`. The 1.5 is tolerance on the Gaussian `√(2/π)` approximation,
         which the atom skews — it is not a physical claim. This assertion is the sharp guard the
         withdrawn AC2.10 was reaching for and failed to be: it is anchored to the realized data
         rather than to an assumed structural property, it fails loudly if F3 reverts to the
         `φ̄`-divided cache or otherwise stops being the exact conditional mean, and it cannot be
         satisfied by distorting a physical parameter, because distorting one moves the floor and
         the error together.

      **Recorded, not asserted.** qa records in `.team/qa/T-150-tests.md`: the three MAEs, the
      computed floor, `MAE(F3)/floor`, and the implied signal ratio
      `√(MAE(P0)² − MAE(F3)²) / √(MAE(F2)² − MAE(F3)²)`. These are the numbers a future reader
      needs to tell a threshold miss from an estimator problem without re-deriving this section.

      **Diagnostic that settles the F3 finding — mandatory, run once, report the numbers.**
      Run the fixture at `n = 8`, `64` and `256` units per delivery and record `MAE(F3)` at each.
      If it falls as `1/√n` (≈0.042 → ≈0.013 → ≈0.007), F3 is at its Bayes floor, the small
      F3-to-F2 gap is a measurement-resolution artifact, and the r3 finding is confirmed. If it
      plateaus above ≈0.02, **F3 carries a real bias**: that is a defect, it outranks this whole
      criterion, and Phase 2 reopens. Independently and cheaply (no session run): for several
      `Λ ∈ [3, 11]`, `filter_law_mean_f(Exposure(Λ))` must agree with a ≥10⁶-draw Monte Carlo of
      `max(0, 1 − Gamma(k·Λ·ψ, θ))`, `ψ ~ LogNormal(0, sigma_pos)`, to **≤0.005** — tighter than
      AC2.19's stated tolerance, because a 0.005 bias in F3's law is invisible to AC2.19 and would
      consume a fifth of the F2→F3 signal.

      **Still not asserted:** `sd(f | F3) < sd(f | F2)` at a tight tolerance. The F2→F3 *residual
      spread* gain is genuinely small — `φ̄` is ≈1.6% of `Var(log Λ)` (AC2.18) — and ADR 0144
      Correction 1 already ruled that this is a result, not a bug. F2 receiving
      `pack_date_days = duration_d.round()` means part of the F2→F3 tracking gain is de-rounding
      the date; that is legitimate and expected, and it is why the signal ratio comes in nearer 5
      than the 8 that the raw 98.4% duration share would suggest.

## Interfaces

Generative model (ADR 0144 §3), all in f-space:

```
d       = d_min + Gamma(a_d, b_d)              per corridor, from arrival_product
T_bar   ~ TruncNormal(mu_T, sigma_T, low=0C)
phi_bar = q10^((T_bar - T_ref)/10)
psi_pos ~ Lognormal(0, sigma_pos)              PER UNIT
Lambda  = d * phi_bar * psi_pos                cumulative thermal exposure, reference-days
D       ~ Gamma(k * Lambda, theta)             PER UNIT
f       = max(0, 1 - D)

P(f > x | Lambda) = gamma_p(k*Lambda, (1 - x) / theta)
P(f = 0 | Lambda) = gamma_q(k*Lambda, 1 / theta)      exact, never grid mass
```

## Implementation constraints

These are binding on the implementer; a reviewer should reject a diff that violates them.

- **Cache shape follows what the rung observes.** Mirror `GammaDecrementTable`
  (`physics.rs:262-301`): fixed 4096-point grid, linear interpolation, built once via a
  `for_params`-style constructor. One table does **not** fit all rungs. P0/P1: the mixture over
  `d` and `T_bar` is fully collapsible, so pre-mix **one** 1D CDF over `f`, built once.
  F2/F2a: `pack_date_days` is `Option<i32>` (`obs.rs:58`), so cache a small map keyed by that
  integer — cheap and exact. F3: `Λ` is continuous, so pre-mixing is impossible; keep a table
  over `Λ` and interpolate. F3 is the *cheapest* rung to build, not the most expensive: with
  `Λ` known, only `ψ_pos` and the gamma remain.
- **RNG streams: add new names, never repurpose old ones.** `SpawnRng::spawn_rng` addresses
  streams by hashing the stream name (`spawn_rng.rs:23-31`), so adding a stream costs nothing
  and perturbs nothing. Give each new quantity its own stream (`:arrival_duration`,
  `:arrival_temp`, `:arrival_pos`, `:arrival_gamma`) and **retire** `ARRIVAL_SHIP` /
  `ARRIVAL_SENSOR` rather than redefining what they draw, so review can see exactly which
  goldens are expected to move. Demand / spoil / alloc goldens must stay bit-identical.
- **Do not introduce a third RNG addressing scheme.** Two already exist: string-named
  `SpawnRng` (`rollout.rs:14-18`, `alpha_tune.rs:19`) and numeric `stream_rng`
  (`session.rs:27`, `voi.rs:23-30`). Add the new streams consistently to both; add no third.
- **`ψ_pos` and the gamma draw are per unit, not per delivery.** Drawing them per delivery
  reproduces the exact bug being fixed.
- **Embed the artifact exactly once.** `demand_profile.json` is `include_str!`'d at six-plus
  sites (`params.rs:75`, `demand_profile.rs:121`, `alpha_tune.rs:456`, `physics.rs:658,690`,
  `voi.rs:548`, `session.rs:815`). Do not replicate that pattern.
- **Quadrature nodes and weights are deterministic and version-pinned in the artifact**, or
  Python/Rust parity drifts for reasons unrelated to the model.
- **No new runtime dependencies.**

## Risks the next roles should know

1. **The "14×" is branch-dependent.** The `eta_ref` division makes transit 14× too cheap on the
   pack-date and temperature-history branches, but the P0/P1 default branch reconstructs
   `delivery_lambda` by dividing by `k·θ = 0.16` first (`day_step.rs:270-276`), so it nets out at
   ≈2.24× too cheap. Do not write a test asserting a uniform 14× correction across all rungs.
2. **The recalibration and the convention switch move numbers together.** Isolate them: AC2.3
   pins the shape-scaling spread at the *old* parameters, AC2.4 pins the new parameters. Landing
   them in one commit without that separation makes every golden move for two reasons at once
   and makes the diff unreviewable.
3. **Three review rounds is the escalation limit.** Phase boundaries are the mitigation. If
   Phase 2 alone exceeds one failed round, stop and split it rather than pushing through.
4. **A criterion can be wrong, and a green suite will not tell you.** AC2.10 was unsatisfiable
   at physically honest parameters, so passing it required `sigma_T = 3.6` °C — a refrigerated
   reefer running anywhere from freezing to 10 °C. Every gate stayed green. If a criterion can
   only be met by moving a *physical* parameter well outside what the data shows, the criterion
   is the thing that is broken. Say so and stop; do not tune the artifact to the test.
   **And the mirror image is equally available:** a criterion that a correct model misses may be
   right about the effect and wrong about the *measurement*, in which case relaxing the threshold
   destroys the guard while leaving the real defect — an estimator too coarse to see the effect —
   in place for the next ticket to inherit. AC2.11a in correction round 2 was this case: the 3×
   gate was unreachable not because the ladder is flat but because an 8-unit lot mean carries a
   0.053 noise floor against a 0.025 signal. Before relaxing any threshold, decompose the
   measurement and state the noise floor. If the floor is the same order as the effect, the
   estimator is the thing to fix.
5. **Phase 3's recalibration is the long pole**, not the code. α tuning, VOI CRN and two
   notebooks all regenerate. Budget for it, and do not let a stale committed α table pass as
   "unchanged".
