      `day` is provided and a profile is configured, the NB mean equals profile μ(day); when `day`
      is `None` and no profile, behaviour matches pre-CAL constant `demand_mu` (compat).

      **JSON only** (no HF).

      equivalent); CRN call sites can pass episode day.

      `[freshnet]` installed.
- [ ] Two different weekdays with distinct profile means produce different expected μ (test with
      seeded RNG distribution check or direct μ accessor).

- [ ] Focused pytest + ruff/mypy on touched paths pass; any A2 shim tests remain green.
